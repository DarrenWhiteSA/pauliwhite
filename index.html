01 / Introduction
PAULI WHITE sensory gardens

Gardens designed to be experienced.

Bringing together architectural elements, sensory planting and thoughtful details to create beautiful, considered outdoor spaces.

Cape Town, South Africa.

02 / Approach

Structure. Senses. Self.

I design gardens around three things: the architectural elements that shape a space, the plants and details that engage the senses, and the way people interact with and experience their surroundings.

01

Structure

The architectural elements of a garden.

Natural stone, paving, pools, pathways, walls and built features that give a space its form and character.

02

Senses

The natural and tactile elements that bring a garden to life.

Layered planting, texture, colour, fragrance, movement, water and thoughtful sensory accessories.

03

Self

The person experiencing the garden.

How we move, explore, play, gather, pause and connect with our surroundings.

03 / Process

From idea to garden.

  2. 02

    Concept

    A site visit and initial conceptualisation to establish the design direction and project scope. R1,500, credited against the professional fee if the project proceeds.

  3. 03

    Design

    Refining the materials, planting, details and project estimate.

  4. 04

    Installation

    Sourcing, coordination and installation. A 60% deposit is payable before procurement, with the remaining 40% at handover.
