The studio

Gardens that reward slow attention: restrained in form, rich in detail, and planted to change beautifully with every season.

Pauli White is a landscape design studio led by Pauli White, trained in landscape architecture. The work begins with the land as it is: its light, soil, views and the way people will move through it. From there, every space is shaped through touch, scent, sound, sight and taste.

Each commission is carried personally from first sketch to masterplan, planting design, procurement and installation, so the finished landscape holds true to the idea that began it.

Who we design for

Places with a sense of arrival

  1. i.

    Private estates & residences

    Arrival courts, garden rooms, natural pools and planting that frames the architecture and the view beyond.

  2. ii.

    Hotels, lodges & guest houses

    Restorative outdoor spaces that become part of the guest experience, from courtyards and spa gardens to gathering places around fire and water.

  3. iii.

    Wine farms

    Tasting-room gardens, edible and aromatic borders, and waterwise landscapes that sit naturally against vineyard and mountain.

  4. iv.

    Schools & early learning

    Sensory play gardens with stepping-stone trails, water, sand and planting for discovery, alongside calm spaces for children to settle.

  5. v.

    Wellness & practices

    Quiet therapeutic gardens for clinics, retreats and practices, where a landscape supports calm and recovery.

The sensory signature
TouchScentSoundSightTaste

Grasses that move in the wind, fynbos that releases scent as you pass, water over stone, light filtering through a canopy, and fruit within reach. Every landscape is designed through all five senses.

Approach

One studio, start to finish

I

Reading the site

A site visit and measured survey of light, soil, views and how the space will be used.

II

Concept & masterplan

Hand-drawn sketches and concept vignettes that set the atmosphere before the detail.

III

Documentation

Planting plans, working drawings, construction details and bills of quantities.

IV

Installation & handover

Procurement, supplier coordination and site supervision through to a considered handover.

Pauli White

Trained in landscape architecture, led by feeling

Pauli trained in landscape architecture at the University of Pretoria and later in interior design. She has worked as a landscape technologist on planting plans, renders, bills of quantities and installations, and as an in-house plant stylist. Her work is shaped by a long-held interest in how our surroundings affect wellbeing.
